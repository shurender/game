# Narrative & Story Design (`story/`)

This directory documents the world lore, narrative progression, character design sheets, and quest arcs for the game.

---

## Story Overview & Main Arc

The story begins in the peaceful village of **Oakhaven**:
1. **Act 1: The Journey Begins (`main_01`)**
   - The protagonist wakes up at home and speaks with **Mom**, who mentions that **Professor Cedar** is waiting next door at his research laboratory.
   - Reward: 3 Potions to prepare for the wild road.
2. **Act 2: Special Delivery (`main_02`)**
   - Professor Cedar entrusts the protagonist with their first partner creature (**Florbit**) and hands over a sealed **Parcel** destined for **Mayor Riverbend** in Riverbend City.
   - The player journeys through **Route 1**, battling wild creatures and local trainers (Youngster Joey, Lass Mia).
3. **Act 3: Rogue Menace (`main_03`)**
   - Upon arriving in Riverbend City and delivering the parcel, the Mayor reveals that a rogue syndicate has blocked **Route 2**.
   - The player ventures through the dark **Cave** to Route 2, confronting **Rogue Leader Boss**, overcoming the threat and earning a bounty of Capture Orbs.
